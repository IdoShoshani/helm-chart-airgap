How it works
  1. Enter your Helm chart source (repository or OCI registry)
  2. Optionally provide values.yaml to customize the chart
  3. Click "Package Chart" — with images enabled, you'll first select which images to include (from discovered images + optional custom list)
  4. You'll see a progress page with a live progress bar
  5. The tool downloads the chart, selected images, and packages everything into a single bundle
  6. Download the bundle and transfer it to your air-gapped environment
